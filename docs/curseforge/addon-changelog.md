# GTIBD Addon 0.0.1

- Packages the 504 KubeJS files used in GregTech: Industrial Brain Damage 0.0.1.
- Includes the recipe and registry scripts, client customizations, item and quest textures, and English and Russian item names.
- Adds the ZPM and UV acceptance crates, reserve bleed record, one pressure record per dimension and the second simultaneous-load record.
- Includes the final commissioning logic and the ordered assembly recipe with 15 item inputs and four fluids.
- Adds an installation guide for replacing the KubeJS folder in the matching pack, with backup instructions for profiles and worlds.

Use with GregTech: Industrial Brain Damage 0.0.1 on Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.4.28.
